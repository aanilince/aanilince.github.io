Common questions

The number flashes too fast to read. That is the game. Rounds start slow and speed up; the count-in beat before each flash tells you exactly when to look.

I bought Remove Ads and still see ads. Open the home screen and tap Restore Purchase, then restart the app. If ads remain, email us with the purchase date.

I want my purchase on another device. Sign in to the same Apple Account on the other device and tap Restore Purchase.

Can I play offline? Yes. Only ads need a connection.
