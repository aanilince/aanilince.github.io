Eyesight Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Eyesight ("the app") is a reflex and memory game published by Anıl İnce. This page explains what the app does with data.

What the app stores

Eyesight has no accounts and no sign-in. Scores, titles, player names typed in party mode and your settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded by the app.

Advertising

The free version shows interstitial ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect the device advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location derived from it, and interaction data to serve and measure ads. On iOS the app asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency before any personalised ad is shown; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. Where required, a consent form is shown before ads load. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Buying "Remove Ads" removes the ad SDK's activity from the app entirely.

Ad measurement

The developer advertises the app, for example on TikTok. To learn which ad led to an install, the app uses AppsFlyer, an ad measurement service, and shares the results with TikTok. AppsFlyer may collect the device advertising identifier (only if you allow it), the identifier for vendors, IP address, country, device and install details, and two in-app events: the first game you finish and the third game you finish. Each event is sent once. AppsFlyer's privacy policy: https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy/ TikTok's privacy policy: https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy

The app asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency after you finish your first game. If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared. Apple's SKAdNetwork may still report anonymous campaign results, and AppsFlyer may still send TikTok install and event information for devices without an advertising identifier. Where required, the consent form shown before ads also covers this measurement.

Buying "Remove Ads" also stops this measurement from then on.

Game settings

Each time you open the app, it asks Firebase Remote Config, a Google service, for game settings such as how often ads appear. The request also happens if you bought "Remove Ads". Firebase creates a random installation identifier that belongs to this install of the app only; it is not your advertising identifier. Firebase receives this identifier together with your country and language, time zone, operating system version, the app's bundle identifier and the app and SDK version. No name, email address, score or advertising identifier is sent, and Firebase Analytics is not used. Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Purchases

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. To restore the purchase on another device, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and the purchase receipt. No name, email or payment details reach the app or RevenueCat. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Daily challenge sharing

Sharing a daily result uses the iOS share sheet with a short text you can see before sending. Nothing is sent by the app itself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

Updates to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
